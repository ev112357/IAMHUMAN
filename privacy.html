Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Human-Only Forum ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your unique username and email address to manage authentication and profile settings.
  • User Content: Forum posts, thread topics, direct messages, group chat messages, and attached image files uploaded to the platform.
  • Security & Anti-Bot Telemetry: To prevent automated spam and unauthorized automated scraping, our system analyzes client-side typing cadence, interaction timers, and composition telemetry. This data is evaluated in real-time to distinguish humans from automated bots and is not sold.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and maintain your user account and personal profile.
  • To display your posts, messages, and uploaded photos within forum threads and private chats.
  • To protect our community against automated bot networks, spam, and abuse.
  • To notify you of incoming messages, friend requests, and system updates.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your authentication credentials and stored content are managed using encrypted, industry-standard database protocols. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Account & Data Deletion (Your Rights)

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete your account at any time directly inside the app by navigating to Profile Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account. When you confirm deletion, your account credentials, profile avatar, friend connections, and associated personal records are permanently erased from our systems.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, you may reach out to us at our support portal or via email at support@yourforum.com.
